# A specific question or exploration title

> Status: seed
> Date: YYYY-MM-DD
> Scope: the system, boundary, or use case under discussion
> Related issue: add its URL when one exists

## question

What are we trying to understand, and why is it worth investigating?

## context and boundaries

What motivates the question? Which existing notes or projects does it relate to?
What is in scope, explicitly out of scope, or still an assumption?

## constraints and invariants

What must remain true? Where are the trust, ownership, state, and failure boundaries?
Distinguish desired properties from capabilities already verified in a specific version.

## candidate approaches

Compare meaningful alternatives, including the simplest useful baseline.
Explain tradeoffs, missing primitives, and what evidence would disqualify each option.

## smallest useful experiment

Describe a bounded experiment or worked example, its setup and version pins,
observable outcomes, failure cases, and what the result would and would not establish.
Do not present an experiment plan as an experiment result.

## open questions

Identify the questions that materially affect the design. Record what would change
your mind, what can be deferred, and what needs evidence rather than more discussion.

## references and evidence

Prefer primary sources. Record inspected repository refs/commits and the inspection
date for capability claims. Mark inference and proposal explicitly.

## outcome and follow-through

Leave this section out until there is an outcome. Summarize findings, remaining
uncertainty, and links to follow-up work, accepted decisions, or the reason to archive.
